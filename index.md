I am an organic chemist working at the interface of **natural products chemistry, food biochemistry, and plant genetic resources**. My research focuses on the extraction, purification, identification, and quantification of bioactive metabolites, especially flavonoids, isoflavones, and other polyphenols, from food crops and medicinal plants, and on how genotype, origin, and seed traits shape their nutritional and antioxidant properties.

Since February 2026 I have been an Assistant Professor at DGIST. Before that, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the National Agrobiodiversity Center, National Institute of Agricultural Sciences, Rural Development Administration (RDA), Jeonju, Korea. I have more than a decade of university teaching experience in general, organic, and medicinal chemistry at Adama Science and Technology University, Mekelle University, and Wollega University in Ethiopia.
